Meet the Crew

Five people who work at Boom Town, a small gold-rush theme park built over an old silver mine in the Colorado mountains, and who keep running into murders that look impossible.

Agnes Penhallow

Twenty-two, five foot two, and the only person in Silver Hollow who has never once lost her temper in public. Agnes runs Guest Resolution, which is the complaints desk, from a one-room office dressed up as the sheriff's. Every case comes to her as a complaint. She has a notebook, thirty keys on a lanyard, one blue eye and one green, and a voice that makes furious people calm down and start explaining. Nobody suspects her of being clever. That's the point.

Bunny Doe

Nobody knows her last name, her age, or where she was before she came to the Hollow. She runs the old-timey photo studio in summer and the fudge wagon on the road, carries the propane tanks herself, and keeps lemon cookies in her cardigan pockets, each in its own napkin. Grown men tell her things. She tells them about the weather. "Sweetheart," she says, right before something devastating.

Buckley

Benji Mathers, twenty-six, is Buckley the Beaver, the park's mascot, and answers to Buckley faster than to Benji. Inside the suit the rules are simple and he is the biggest thing on Main Street. Outside it he's quiet, exact, and kind. He has the whole park in his head as a map, every tunnel and door and timetable, and he notices when one thing is in the wrong place. Nobody looks at the mascot. So the mascot sees everything.

Mags Lowe

Nineteen, neon hair (the colour changes), headphones with lights, and a laptop so covered in stickers it has no visible surface. Mags taught herself the park's 1980s ride-control system from the manuals and now runs the whole place from a laptop it should not be able to run on. She says she hates the park and wants out. Nobody believes her, and she has the data to prove they're wrong, which somehow doesn't help. Speaks in patch notes. Hates the word "vibes".

Dash Reyes

Twenty-eight, dimples, a hat for every occasion, and always slightly overdressed. Dash does the Boom Town Showdown four times a day, writes more of the comedy show than anyone knows, and has a nickname for everyone, including the sheriff. Everyone tells him things. He tells them a story about his suite at the grand hotel. He flirts with Agnes. It's a bit. Probably.

Nugget

A raccoon who lives under the log flume and steals evidence, mostly edible. Agnes leaves a dish out for him. This was a mistake she has decided to keep making.

Pyrite

A fox who looks like gold and is trouble. Steals anything shiny and brings it to Buckley, which is why there is often a tourist's earring inside the beaver. Nugget steals food. Pyrite steals light. They fight constantly and then act like best friends.
