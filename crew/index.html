Personnel files

Meet the Crew

Five people who work at Boom Town, a small gold-rush theme park built over an old silver mine in the Colorado mountains, and who keep running into murders that look impossible.

Agnes Penhallow

Twenty-two, five foot two, and the only person in Silver Hollow who has never once lost her temper in public. Agnes runs Guest Resolution, which is the complaints desk, from a one-room office dressed up as the sheriff's. Every case comes to her as a complaint. She has a notebook, thirty keys on a lanyard, one blue eye and one green, and a voice that makes furious people calm down and start explaining. Nobody suspects her of being clever. That's the point.

Bunny Doe

Nobody knows her last name, her age, or where she was before she came to the Hollow. She runs the old-timey photo studio in summer and the fudge wagon on the road, carries the propane tanks herself, and keeps lemon cookies in her cardigan pockets, each in its own napkin. Grown men tell her things. She tells them about the weather. "Sweetheart," she says, right before something devastating.

Buckley

Benji Mathers, twenty-six, is Buckley the Beaver, the park's mascot, and answers to Buckley faster than to Benji. Inside the suit the rules are simple and he is the biggest thing on Main Street. Outside it he's quiet, exact, and kind. He has the whole park in his head as a map, every tunnel and door and timetable, and he notices when one thing is in the wrong place. Nobody looks at the mascot. So the mascot sees everything.

Mags Lowe

Nineteen, neon hair (the colour changes), headphones with lights, and a laptop so covered in stickers it has no visible surface. Mags taught herself the park's 1980s ride-control system from the manuals and now runs the whole place from a laptop it should not be able to run on. She says she hates the park and wants out. Nobody believes her, and she has the data to prove they're wrong, which somehow doesn't help. Speaks in patch notes. Hates the word "vibes".

Dash Reyes

Twenty-eight, dimples, a hat for every occasion, and always slightly overdressed. Dash does the Boom Town Showdown four times a day, writes more of the comedy show than anyone knows, and has a nickname for everyone, including the sheriff. Everyone tells him things. He tells them a story about his suite at the grand hotel. He flirts with Agnes. It's a bit. Probably.

Nugget

A raccoon who lives under the log flume and steals evidence, mostly edible. Agnes leaves a dish out for him. This was a mistake she has decided to keep making.

Pyrite

A fox who looks like gold and is trouble. Steals anything shiny and brings it to Buckley, which is why there is often a tourist's earring inside the beaver. Nugget steals food. Pyrite steals light. They fight constantly and then act like best friends.

Around the Hollow

The people the crew keeps running into in Hall of Broken Mirrors. Everyone has a reason to lie.

Deputy Cole Whitaker

Thirty-two or so, nine years a deputy, and one of the two the county keeps in Silver Hollow. He has the kind of face that was handsome in high school and is now mostly tired, a hat he takes off when the news is bad, and firm views about complaints girls who open murder investigations from a desk. He calls Agnes "Ms. Penhallow". He is usually right about procedure and usually wrong about who did it, and he would like that to stop happening in front of her.

Hollis Bascombe

Forty-four, grandson of the man who built Boom Town in 1962, and the park's heir. Hollis put electronic locks on every door, shut the old gift shop because a kiosk with one teenager in it sells just as much, and believed in doing the math. Then one Tuesday night he walked into his own park and didn't come home.

Marnie Bascombe

Hollis's wife. Forty-six, linen shirts that cost more than Agnes's car, and the first person to call Guest Resolution about a missing husband, twice, before nine in the morning. She wants someone to do something. She would also like everyone to stop asking where she was.

Professor Gideon Pryce

Seventy-four, retired from the Colorado School of Mines, and in his thirty-eighth summer at the Ambrose, writing a history of the Silver Hollow Mine that nobody has seen a page of. He carries a straw hat and a canvas bag for rock samples, keeps forty pounds of granite in his hotel room, and stops by Agnes's office every week just to say good morning. He courted her grandmother for twenty summers. He is the Hollow's favourite guest, and he knows every rock in it by name.

Cass Bascombe

Hollis's younger sister, forty, who keeps the books and has carried the same clipboard for as long as Agnes has worked at the park, which is since Agnes was sixteen. Cass knows what every ride costs to run and what every ticket brings in, and she is the reason the lights are still on. She does not cry in front of staff. She doesn't put the clipboard down, either.

Royce Tanner

Fifty, tanned, and wearing a shirt that was pressed this morning. Royce is the land agent for the molybdenum mine over the ridge, a mining engineer under the business card, and he has a map with the park circled in red. He would very much like to buy something. He won't say how far he's got.

Wade Keller

Thirty, thin, and growing a goatee to look older. Wade used to run rides at Boom Town and now runs the Lantern Walk ghost tour, in a black vest with the name stitched on it in a font that was supposed to look old. He sells "authentic" miner's lanterns on the side and has the soot to the wrists to prove it. He leaflets the whole town every Thursday and has opinions about the mine.
