About the author

Get to know me

Everly Wylder holds a master’s degree in writing and has attended a writing academy for ten years. She grew up on Quincy, M.E. and Murder, She Wrote, and has loved a caper movie and a good whodunit ever since. She lives in a cabin on an island in the Pacific Northwest, where every morning starts with tea and orange juice and a blueberry muffin while she plots her next mystery. She collects vintage quilts, owls, and colorful novelty lights.

I write the kind of mystery I grew up watching on a Sunday night: a small town, a crime that looks impossible, a sleuth nobody takes seriously, and a crew of friends who turn out to be the whole point. The ghosts in my books are sad rather than scary, the murders happen off the page, and there is always fudge.

When I'm not writing I'm hunting for vintage quilts, adding to a collection of owls that has frankly got out of hand, and switching on a string of novelty lights for no reason at all.

Ask Everly

Why cozy mysteries? Because I like puzzles with people in them. A good whodunit is fair: every clue is on the page, and if you're sharper than Agnes you'll get there first.

Why a theme park? A theme park is a small town that's pretending to be another small town. Costumes, tunnels, timetables, and a hundred people who all have a reason to be somewhere else. It's the perfect murder box.

Is there romance? A complication, not a plot. The flirting is a bit. Right up until it isn't.

Where should I start? With Hall of Broken Mirrors. The crew meets there, and so do you.

Will you ever tell us Bunny's last name? Sweetheart. Have a cookie.
