The Boom Town Crew · book 1

Hall of Broken Mirrors

A Paranormal Cozy Mystery

Coming soon

A family of four saw a murder in the mirror maze. There's no body.

Agnes Penhallow works Guest Resolution at Boom Town, a gold-rush theme park built over a played-out silver mine in Silver Hollow, Colorado. She is twenty-two, hyper-organized, and so nice that furious people apologize to her. When the family in matching shirts storms in to report a lantern, a man on a floor, and a second man standing over him, she opens a ticket. Complaint: the maze showed them a murder. Resolution: pending.

By nightfall the park's heir hasn't come home.

The case lands on the one desk in the park that can't say no, and four co-workers who've barely spoken are about to become her crew: a beaver mascot who remembers every door in the park, a ride-control whiz who runs the whole place from a laptop, a showdown cowboy with a past in Vegas, and a fudge-shop grandma who is not, whatever she tells people, seventy-four. Add a raccoon who steals evidence, a fox who steals anything shiny, a widow with a story, a land agent with a map, a ghost-tour guide with soot to his wrists, and the town's favorite harmless old professor, who brings Agnes coffee every week.

Everyone has a reason to lie. None of them is the murder. The answer is under everyone's feet, and it has been for thirty years.

  • A fair-play whodunit with clues on the page and a twist you won't see coming
  • A found-family crew of five, each with a job the case can't be solved without
  • A cozy setting with fudge, funnel cake, old mine shafts and one lantern nobody can explain
  • No gore, no swearing, and the romance is strictly a complication

Hall of Broken Mirrors is the first book in The Boom Town Crew, a paranormal cozy mystery series. If you like Scooby-Doo with a notebook, Murder, She Wrote with a mascot, and small-town secrets with a little something strange in the glass, step right up.
