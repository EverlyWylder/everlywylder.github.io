One-line bio

Everly Wylder writes paranormal cozy mysteries from a cabin on a Pacific Northwest island, with a master’s in writing behind her and tea, orange juice and a blueberry muffin close by.

Short bio

Everly Wylder holds a master’s degree in writing and has attended a writing academy for ten years. She grew up on Quincy, M.E. and Murder, She Wrote, and has loved a caper movie and a good whodunit ever since. She lives in a cabin on an island in the Pacific Northwest, where every morning starts with tea and orange juice and a blueberry muffin while she plots her next mystery. She collects vintage quilts, owls, and colorful novelty lights.
